### [Application Systems (1803ICT)](https://www.griffith.edu.au/study/courses/application-systems-1803ICT)

This course focuses on the analysis, integration, and design of socio-technical systems. Key areas include:
- **User Experience (UX)** and interface design principles.
- **Contextual considerations** for e-health, e-business, and e-government platforms.
- **System Modelling** and understanding organizational computing contexts.

As a Teaching Assistant, I facilitate tutorials and labs, helping students understand the principles of socio-technical systems and guiding them through the process of designing complete application systems.
