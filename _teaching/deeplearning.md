### [Deep Learning (3008ICT)](https://www.griffith.edu.au/study/courses/deep-learning-3008ICT)

This course provides a comprehensive exploration of deep learning fundamentals and practical applications. Key topics include:
- **Neural Networks**, including CNNs and RNNs.
- **Deep Learning Frameworks** like TensorFlow and PyTorch.
- **Practical applications** in computer vision and natural language processing.

As a Teaching Assistant, I help students bridge the gap between theory and hands-on knowledge, providing guidance on building and training robust deep learning models from the ground up.
