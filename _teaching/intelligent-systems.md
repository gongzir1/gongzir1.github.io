### [Intelligent Systems (2802ICT)](https://www.griffith.edu.au/study/courses/intelligent-systems-2802ICT)

This course introduces the foundations and practical development of intelligent computational systems. It develops students' understanding of how artificial-intelligence methods can be used to represent knowledge, reason about problems, learn from data, and build systems that make informed decisions.

As a Teaching Assistant, I facilitate practical classes and support students with the implementation, testing, and evaluation of intelligent-system techniques. I also help students connect theoretical AI concepts with hands-on problem solving and provide constructive feedback on their work.
